Datapath Cluster Tetris

Winner on x0: -4.4 um2, D2 better, robust to local perturbation; small

The 813 datapath cells of the shipping netlist x0 were re-placed starting from the tool's own x0 placement. The 216 flops and every non-datapath cell stay at their tool sites. 597 combinational dp cells were moved by a legal-move annealer on repair- and criticality-weighted wire length, plus a compaction term. All 1,362 synthesized cells are held FIRM (MACRO_PLACEMENT_CFG) with the resizer's upsizes reserved in place. Packing was tightened one weight at a time (D8c) and converged at 86.6% (seed 83.3%). The winner xpk20_s1 is 4.4 um2 below x0 at signoff, all from timing-repair buffers.

Decision

xpk20_s1 signs off at 44,907.2 um2 against x0's 44,911.6 (-4.4), slew/cap/fanout 189/1/0 against 242/1/0, setup +42.31 ns, hold +0.034 ns (x0 +0.007), DRC/LVS/antenna 0, all FIRM cells held at all 16 step DEFs. The harden reproduced the dry run exactly. The gain is entirely repair buffers (_1222_ needs 2 buffers instead of 3, -22.0; _1085_ and _1311_ get buf_4 instead of buf_3, +17.6). Upsizing (805.6) and the clock tree (759.5) are unchanged. Two perturbed variants (13 and 10 cells re-annealed) also give 44,907.2. The recipe is not seed-robust: other annealer seeds give +17.6 or fail cap. Of the structural terms, bit slices (+59.3), hinge targets (+39.5 / +70.2) and the ring array (+17.6) lost; only compaction won.
