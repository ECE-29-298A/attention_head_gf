Chip partition B pilot 2: every cell custom-placed (FIRM) in floorplan B

Rejected as a chip candidate (D2 slew/cap, +109.7 um2); mechanism proven at full scale

Full-scale test of the M-FIRM integration mechanism. All 1,416 non-tie cells of the frozen h2 netlist are placed by tools/chip_place.py into floorplan B (spi from pilot 1, then dp, ctrl and div with simulated annealing). They are made FIRM through MACRO_PLACEMENT_CFG, the only change from the h2 config. Space for the resizer's 50 in-place upsizes is reserved in advance from a standalone probe of steps 17-32. Result: all 1,416 cells end FIXED where they were placed (0 moved, 0 mirrored), the 50 upsizes all fall within their reservations, and GPL moved only the 18 ties. Magic DRC, route DRC, LVS and antenna are all 0, and setup and hold are legal at all 9 corners. Every region reaches the D8 target: spi 89.2%, ctrl 90.8%, div 90.0%, dp 86.0% (all non-fill cells plus taps over the region area). The candidate is rejected on quality, not mechanism: std-cell area is 45,585.5 against 45,475.8 for h2 (+109.7 um2), and max_ss slew/cap violations rise to 474/3 against 281/2 (D2). The cause is wirelength: my placer's HPWL is 35% above GPL (55,032 against 40,659 um), and routed wire is 73,427 against 50,554 um. The standalone dry run of steps 17-55 reproduced this harden exactly (area, slew/cap/fanout, setup, hold), so later cluster placements can be judged without a harden.

Decision

Keep M-FIRM as the integration mechanism. Its all-custom form is now proven end to end at about 90% region packing with signoff clean. Do not use this placement: my quick annealing placer loses to GPL+DPL on wirelength, which adds repair buffers (+109.8 um2) and max_ss slew violations. Pilot 2 sets the bar the tetrising agents must clear. Judge their cluster placements with the standalone dry run through step 55 (scripts/pilot_dryrun.py --through 55): std-cell area below 45,475.8, slew/cap/fanout within 281/2/6, and setup and hold >= 0.
