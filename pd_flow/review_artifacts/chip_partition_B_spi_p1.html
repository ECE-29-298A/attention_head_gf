Chip partition B pilot: SPI block custom-placed (FIRM) inside the h2 chip

Mechanism proven; block kept for chip assembly

Integration-mechanism pilot for the all-custom-placed chip. The 32 flat cells attributed to the SPI wrapper are placed by tools/region_place.py into a 4-row region (237.44..342.16 x 137.20..152.88 um) under the host SPI pins, at 83.6% packing (cells + taps) before the flow and 89.2% after in-place upsizing, which uses the whitespace reserved for it. MACRO_PLACEMENT_CFG makes them FIRM at step 17; nothing else in the h2 config changes. The reservations come from a deterministic dry run of steps 17-32 (resizer decisions are status-agnostic) and converged in one iteration: the same 6 upsizes, all within the reserved space. Result: the 32 cells end FIXED at their exact positions (0 moved, 0 mirrored), DRC/LVS/antenna clean, slew/cap/fanout below h2, timing legal. Std-cell area 45,467.0 against 45,475.8 (-8.8 um2): within the ~400 um2 harden noise, so the area effect is inconclusive. The SPI cells' upsizing (92.2 um2) and repair buffers (149.3 um2) are identical to h2, because they are driven by the block's long nets to GPL-placed neighbours, not by its internal layout.

Decision

Adopt M-FIRM (MACRO_PLACEMENT_CFG + upsizing reservations from a standalone dry run) as the integration mechanism for the cluster placements. The SPI placement is not an area gain on its own (-8.8 um2, inconclusive); it is kept as the spi region of floorplan B and re-placed when its neighbours are placed.
