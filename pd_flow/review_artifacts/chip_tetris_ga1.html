Whole-Chip Tetris

Winner ga1: -35.1 um2 (CTS), slew 146 -> 115, signoff = dry run

Every synthesized cell of the chip is placed together, starting from the tool's own placement, and handed to the flow FIXED with the r32 handoff: steps 17-32 run as in the reference, so repair and upsizing still happen at the tool's GPL positions, and the placement is fixed right after RepairDesignPostGPL (LibreLane meta.substituting_steps). This handoff reproduces wc_ref2 exactly, with zero FIRM penalty; FIRM at step 17 costs +61.5 to +74.7. With repair fixed, the area lever is the clock tree. CTS pads every leaf whose clock-pin cap is below its subtree's largest leaf with a dummy load, so the dummy area depends only on how the flops are clustered. A greedy search with the real CTS in the loop chose the flop set: the tool's final flop positions plus one relocation of _2062_. A chip-wide anneal of the 1,007 non-flop cells, weighted by repair and criticality, then cut slew violations. Winner ga1 signs off at 43,394.7 um2, 35.1 below wc_ref2, all of it CTS.

Decision

ga1 signs off at 43,394.7 um2 against wc_ref2's 43,429.8 (-35.1). Slew/cap/fanout is 115/1/0 against 146/1/0, setup +40.03 ns, hold +0.065 ns (wc_ref2 +0.081), route DRC 0, Magic DRC 0, LVS 0, antenna 0, and the netlist is identical to wc_ref2's. The gain is entirely the clock tree: 777.1 against 812.2, the same 3 clkbuf_8 and 17 leaf clkbuf_4, with leaf dummies inv_4 x2 + inv_3 replacing clkinv_4 x2 + inv_4 + a clkbuf_4. Repair (746.4) and upsizing (289.8, the same 28 cells) are identical by construction. Robustness: two re-annealed variants (41 and 46 cells moved, no flops) give the same 43,394.7, and every one of the 12 non-flop anneals on the two chosen flop sets lands on its set's area. A variant whose flops were disturbed falls back to -21.9: the CTS part of the gain is sensitive to flop moves. Losers: FIRM at step 17 (+30.8 to +74.7), letting the wire annealer move flops (-13.1 / -17.5 only), and compaction (no area change; density converges at 84.0-84.1%).
