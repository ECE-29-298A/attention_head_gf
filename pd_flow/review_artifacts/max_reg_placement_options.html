max_reg Placement Options

Not adopted; superseded by cluster design (D7)

Three ways to place max_reg (22 flops, a signed 22-bit compare and the load mux) were tried in the chip against free placement: (b) only the 22 M flops fixed in a column; (b) the M flops and mac_unit's S flops fixed and bit-aligned; (a) a whole bit-sliced block fixed (sdffq_1 per bit with a compare slice). Every other cell was placed by the tool.

Decision

No constrained option reduced chip area legally. b_m came in 35 µm² under the baseline, inside run-to-run noise, with an identical netlist and 4 cap violations. b_ms and the fixed block were larger and added wire (+8.8 mm and +12.2 mm). The block's own cells (2,952.5 µm²) only match the cells synthesis already gives max_reg inside the chip (2,954.7 µm²), so the loss comes from the rest of the chip being mapped and placed around it. max_reg will be placed together with mac_unit's S register and adder slice. The v2 block (63 × 22 sites, 96.6% packing, 2 split SE drivers) is kept as a building block.
