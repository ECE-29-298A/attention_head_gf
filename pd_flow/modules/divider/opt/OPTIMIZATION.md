# divider: architectural area optimization (Agent 3, provisional)

Date: 2026-11-26. **Recommendation: c06 (`opt/best/divider.v`, SHA-256 `9e6530df…`).** It is
EQY-proven equivalent to the golden at (LATCH_DENOMINATOR, CHECK_INVALID) = (1,1) and (0,0), after
power-on reset (see "Proof method"). It passes lint and the existing divider and controller tests.
Under the shipping x0 flow it lowers the in-context divider cone by 676 µm² (8,423.0 → 7,746.9,
−8.0%) and chip synthesis area by 367 µm². Every cosmetic variant of c06 is below the golden noise band.

All results are provisional: the frozen contract does not exist yet. `verif/` and `review/` were
not read. c06 relies on **no caller guarantee**. Its equivalence holds for every input sequence,
including a denominator that changes mid-division at (0,0), zero denominators, sum_in = −524288 and
out-of-range quotients. The one premise is that rst_n is asserted once after power-up (the chip
reset). Before that first reset, both the golden and c06 show power-up garbage, and the two need not
agree.

### Area class breakdown (golden → c06)
- **Chip synthesis:**
  - sequential 20,944.4 → 20,498.8 (−445.6, −7 dffq_1);
  - combinational 19,780.9 → 19,860.0 (+79.1).
- **Divider cone:**
  - flops 3,628.7 → 3,183.0 (57 → 50);
  - logic 4,794.3 → 4,563.8 (−230.5): xor2 44 → 27, nor2 41 → 18, mux2_1 10 → 22, nand2 27 → 49.
- **Rest of chip:** 32,214.6 → 32,537.3 (+322.7). This is ABC reshuffling outside the divider: the
  golden's own cosmetic variants move rest by up to +224, and c06's variants by +40 to +402.

  The divider cone saving (−676) is therefore the number to carry into D7 cluster placement. The
  chip-level number (−367, range −329 to −498) is what flat synthesis gives today.

### Where the savings come from
- **c01, 4 flops: dq19.** 16·|O| has four zero LSBs, so the dividend needs only 19 stored bits.
  - At every step s, the register holds (19−s) dividend bits plus at most 12 live quotient bits,
    which never exceeds 19.
  - Quotient bits that would reach the top (s ≥ 19) are masked from the trial. The decode is
    `step[4] & (step[2] | step[1] & step[0])`, which also covers ROUND.
  - The upper quotient bits q0..q3 fall off the top. At (0,0) they are never observed. At
    CHECK_INVALID=1 the overflow check needs q0..q10, so a generate-only sticky flag (`seen`) ORs the
    quotient bits of steps 0..10. That flag does not exist at (0,0).
- **c02, about 12 XORs: flip.** The output stage was `(q ^ neg) + (s ^ neg)`. Storing each quotient
  bit as `subtract ^ negative` when it enters reduces it to `dq + (s ^ neg)`. This is safe because
  quotient bits are never read back as dividend bits. The flip alone (c05) did not help: ABC had
  already shared those XORs in the 23-bit layout. It pays only together with dq19.
- **c06, 2 one-hot state flops plus step==22 logic: state merged into step.** 25 states
  (idle, 23 divide, round) fit in 5 bits. Choosing idle = 24 makes ROUND→IDLE a plain +1, and the
  idle decode is one AND.
  - Keeping the done flop (c04/c06) is better than decoding a separate DONE code (c03): c03 has 1
    flop fewer but +415 rest.
  - Naming the counter `step`, with golden values while busy, keeps the unmodified controller
    testbench working. It probes `dut.divider_unit.step == 3 / 20`. This testbench dependency is
    why c04 is superseded.

### Ideas evaluated and not pursued
- **Removing CHECK_INVALID=0 dead logic.** Nothing is left to remove: invalid_r, overflow and
  `rounded` are already constant-folded by synthesis at (0,0).
- **Shared adder/subtractor, non-restoring division, or dropping the input negation**, for example
  dividing ~X, signed floor division with the remainder initialized to L−1, or folding L/2 into the
  dividend.
  - At (0,0) the divisor is the live `denominator` port, read every step.
  - Unconditional port equivalence therefore forces the same restoring recurrence on the same L
    at every step, and every one of these changes the recurrence.
  - They also differ from the golden at L = 0 and at sum_in = −524288 (the golden gives 0 there).
  - They would need the caller guarantees "denominator stable from start to done, L ≥ 1,
    sum_in ≠ −524288" as EQY assumptions.
  - The estimated gain is about 250–300 µm² (the 19-bit +1 carry chain only; the 19 XORs remain),
    and they need an extra rounding-compare correction (2r'+2 ≥ L). Not worth the conditional
    proof.
- **Result register reuse.** Not possible: the result must hold, and shift, through the whole next
  division, and all 12 result bits are divider ports.
- **LFSR step counter.** The four step decodes (idle, round, ≥ 19, < 11) become scattered minterms.
  The binary +1 costs about 4 half adders. Not tried.

## Proof method (sequential, re-encoded state)
c06 changes the encoding of the quotient register (19 bits, pre-complemented, step-dependent
layout) and of the FSM. Name-matched SAT depth 1 is therefore impossible for it. Two more direct
attempts were rejected:
- Plain k-induction on a single partition with only the ports matched. Yosys SAT stalled at step 34
  of 36 (the golden's free step counter needs about 34 cycles to flush).
- `abc pdr` and `dprove`. Undecided after 25 min and after 60 s.

The proof used instead is a **bridge with observer invariants**, in `bridge/`:
1. `B_gold.v` = golden + observer output ports, and `C_<cand>.v` = candidate + observer ports. The
   original logic is untouched (generated by `make_bridge.py`).
2. **Side proofs** (`run_side.sh`, `eqy_side_P*.eqy`): golden ≡ B and cand ≡ C′. These are EQY at
   P11 and P00, flops and ports matched by name, SAT depth 1, no assumptions. The observer ports are
   demoted to internal wires (`delete -port`), so only the original ports are compared.
3. **Main proof** of B ≡ C′, in the harness `eqtop_obs.v` (identical on both sides):
   - Harness behaviour: `boot` (init 1) forces rst_n low in cycle 0 and masks all ports in cycle 0.
     From cycle 1 every input sequence is free, including later resets.
   - Matching and partitioning: only the harness ports are matched (`*_o`, one partition via
     `[collect] group *_o`). All internal nets are nomatch.
   - The observers make the induction hypothesis carry the invariant:
     - `obs_lg`: the golden state is legal. That means IDLE; or DIVIDE with step ≤ 22 and the four
       zero dividend bits still zero, `(quotient & 4'hF << step) == 0`; or ROUND with step 23. The
       gate side drives 1.
     - `obs_lc`: the candidate step is ≤ 24. The gold side drives 1.
     - `obs_ph`: (state, step) mapped to a common phase.
     - `obs_dq`: the golden quotient mapped to the candidate's 19-bit pre-complemented layout, when
       busy.
     - remainder, negative, divisor, invalid_r and the high-quotient flag, each when busy.
   - **Induction** (`eqy_main_P*.eqy`): Yosys SAT k-induction, depth 2. PASS at P11 and P00.
   - **Base case** (`eqy_base_P*.eqy`): SBY smtbmc z3, xprop on, BMC from the real initial state
     (boot = 1, all other flops free), depth 3 ≥ 2. Pass at P11 and P00.
   - Why both are needed: the Yosys-SAT base case starts from all-undef flops and so is not used,
     while the SBY induction fails spuriously on EQY's X-tolerant compare (`=== 1'bx`).
4. **Composition.** golden ≡ B and C′ ≡ cand hold unconditionally, and B ≡ C′ holds after power-on
   reset. Therefore golden ≡ cand on all original ports from cycle 1 after a power-on reset, for
   every input sequence. The proof is unbounded.

**Negative controls** (`negctl/y0*.v`, mutants of c04 through the same bridge flow). All are
rejected at P00 and P11:
- y01: trial mask starts at step 20 instead of 19;
- y02: sign dropped from the rounding carry;
- y03: start ignored while shift is high;
- y04: result not reset;
- y05: flip lost after step 15.

Each gives induction UNKNOWN. Real counterexamples were found by the base-case BMC:
- y03 at depth 3;
- y02 at depth 30 (a result_o mismatch, `runs/y02_round_no_sign/bridge_base_P00`).

Labels: c06 golden equivalence is **proven** (unbounded, premise: power-on reset), at both parameter
sets. Lint is **pass-static**: the only `-Wall` warning is the golden's own `difference[12]`
UNUSEDSIGNAL, and no new warnings appear. `test/divider/run.py` (default params 1/1, about 110k
vectors) and `test/controller/run.py` (the chip's 0/0) are **pass-sampled**.

## Constraints for the controller-level check
- **No caller guarantee is used.**
- The power-on-reset premise: c06's step counter has a sync reset to 24, and the golden's state has
  a sync reset to IDLE. Before the first rst_n low cycle both are undefined. The chip and controller
  tests always apply reset.
- **Internal name change:** `state` no longer exists. `step` keeps its golden values while busy
  (0..22 divide, 23 round), but in idle it reads 24 instead of 0. Anything that probes
  `divider_unit.state`, or `divider_unit.step` while idle, sees a difference. The existing tests do
  not.
- **Timing:** not measured (no harden). The critical path is the golden's subtract-and-mux path plus
  one decode gate (`step ≥ 19` mask), on a 100 ns clock with +41.8 ns ss slack in the baseline.
